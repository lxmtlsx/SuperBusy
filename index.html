The story

Rome is burning. The sky is full of saucers.

Somewhere between the stars, a Grey transport was hauling a captured little alien puppy toward Earth. Something went wrong on the journey — the ship came down hard, and the puppy slipped out of the wreck and ran. Now he's loose in Rome, and so are the Greys hunting him.

He isn't alone for long. A bulldog of his own species in golden armor, carrying some of the most powerful weapons in the galaxy, shows up between the saucers. The little one calls him SUPER BUSY. His real name? You'll find out in the comic.

Sprint through the ruins of Rome, dodge alien patrols in a neon megacity, grab every coin and orb you can, then board the Bulldog UFO and blast your way through the asteroid belt into orbit. Every run gets you a little closer.

  • 3 heroes, each with an innate power
  • City · Ruins · Orbit — one endless run
  • Story mode — unlock the ship part by part

LUCKY

A little alien from the same bulldog species as Super Busy — no relation, just one of his kind. The Greys captured him and were shipping him to Earth when something went wrong mid-journey; he landed here by accident, slipped out of the wreck and escaped on a red mini-bike. Hoodie up, tongue out, zero fear. Lucky has a nose for anything shiny, and coins seem to fly into his paws on their own. His Crazy Journey has only just begun.
